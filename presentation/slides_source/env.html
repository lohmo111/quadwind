Environment: one control step

Agent

chooses the attitude

Command

thrust + tilt + yaw

Autopilot

drives 4 motors, 500×/s

Physics

motors + wind

Observation, 22 numbers: position error, velocity, orientation, angular rates, previous action

Reward

closer = more; smooth and level = bonus; crash = −50

Episode

15 s = 750 steps; new random disturbances every time

Simulator

our own, NumPy + numba: ~40k steps/s

[Speaker A] Here’s one control step. Fifty times a second the network decides what attitude to take: which way to tilt, how to turn and how much thrust to give. It doesn’t drive the motors directly — an autopilot does that, ten times faster. That’s exactly how a real Crazyflie is controlled. In return the network gets 22 numbers about its state. The reward: closer and smoother is better, and a crash costs minus 50. We wrote the simulator ourselves — it’s fast, with wind, motor lag and delays.
