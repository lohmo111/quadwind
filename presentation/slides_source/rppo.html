[Speaker B] Now put them together. RPPO is PPO with an LSTM in both the actor and the critic. Every step the network takes the new observation plus its memory and decides what to do. That’s how the drone gradually figures out the wind and its own weight. We train on chunks of 512 steps — ten seconds of flight — and push the error back through time: that’s BPTT. And one trick: during training the critic gets a cheat sheet — the true wind and mass. In flight it isn’t needed; only the actor flies.
