Task: hold a point while invisible forces push

A Crazyflie 2.x quadrotor (27 g) must hold a point in the air.

Sees

  • where it is
  • velocity
  • tilt
  • rotation

with noise and delay

Doesn’t see

  • wind up to 3.5 m/s + gusts
  • its mass, ±20–25%
  • a weak motor
  • delays

Disturbances must be inferred from behaviour — that’s the core challenge.

Our simulator: 3 m/s north wind, trajectories of different controllers

[Speaker A] The task sounds simple: hold a point. The drone knows where it is, how fast it’s moving and how it’s tilted — with noise and a small delay. What it doesn’t know is what’s pushing it: wind up to three and a half metres per second, its real mass, a weak motor. All of this is random in every episode. So the drone has to infer the disturbances from how it behaves. On the right is our simulator in a 3 m/s north wind.
