Diagram from the course materials (Lab 7: RNN and LSTM), in the style of StatQuest and C. Olah

[Speaker B] Building block two — LSTM, a network made for time series. Data comes as a stream, fifty frames a second, and the past matters. The green line on top is long-term memory, the cell state: information flows along it almost untouched, so the network remembers seconds, not just a couple of frames. Three gates decide: what to forget — the wind has dropped; what to write — the error persists; and what to output right now. The network learns how to open them by itself. And note: our LSTM doesn’t predict the trajectory — it sits inside the policy and decides what from the past is useful.
